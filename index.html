About the Project

Eco Air is an artificial tree designed to purify the air and increase oxygen levels using IoT and Internet of Sensing technologies. The cost-effective solution targets high-density areas, offering sustainable air quality improvement at a fraction of the cost of real trees. For more details, you can check our Eco Air Drive.
